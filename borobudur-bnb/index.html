Our Story

A Family Home in the Shadow of a Wonder of the World

Borobudur BnB began in 2016 as three joglo-style rooms behind our family's kitchen in Dusun Ngaran — a short walk from the eastern gate of Borobudur Temple. A decade on, we've grown to eight rooms, a garden pool, and a small team of local guides, but the spirit hasn't changed: honest hospitality, home-cooked breakfast, and a front-row seat to the mist rising over the stupas at dawn.

  • Family-run since 2016, four generations in Borobudur village
  • Built with reclaimed teak joglo timber & local volcanic stone
  • Every stay funds our village guide & batik-weaving cooperative
