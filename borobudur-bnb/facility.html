Home—Facility

Built around one sunrise

Every amenity here exists for a reason: to get you rested, fed, and standing in front of Borobudur before the world wakes up.

( 01 ) What's Included

No paywalled amenities, no resort-fee surprises — every room rate already includes the essentials below.

01

Sunrise temple tour

Daily 04:15 guided walk to Borobudur's east gate — included free with every booking.

02

Garden swimming pool

Freshwater pool open 7 am – 9 pm, framed by frangipani trees and rice-field views.

03

Home-cooked breakfast

Served 6:30 – 10 am on the veranda: nasi goreng, tropical fruit, Magelang-grown coffee.

04

Free fibre Wi-Fi

100 Mbps reaching every room, the pool deck and the joglo pavilion.

05

Bicycle & scooter rental

Rp 50K/day bicycles, Rp 90K/day scooters — helmets included, booked at the front desk.

06

Airport & station transfer

Pickup from Yogyakarta Airport (YIA, ~1 hr) or Tugu Station, arranged on request.

07

Laundry service

Same-day wash & fold, Rp 20K/kg — handy after a dusty day exploring the region.

08

Joglo common pavilion

An open-air teak pavilion for breakfast, board games, or an evening beer with other guests.

09

24-hour local hosting

The family lives on-site — someone is always around for questions, big or small.

10

Free private parking

Gated, on-site parking for cars, scooters and tour vans, watched overnight.

11

Batik & village walks

Optional half-day batik workshop and rice-terrace walk with our village cooperative.

12

Travel desk

Train, bus and driver bookings onward to Yogyakarta, Solo, or the Dieng Plateau.

( 02 ) The Sunrise Tour

Four steps to first light

  • 04:15 Wake-up call with hot ginger tea & a torch
  • 04:30 Short walk through the village to the east gate
  • 05:00 Climb to the upper terraces before the crowds
  • 05:45 Sun clears the volcanoes; our guide shares the temple's history

Entry ticket (~Rp 470.000 foreign / Rp 75.000 domestic) is paid at the gate and not included — everything else is on us.
