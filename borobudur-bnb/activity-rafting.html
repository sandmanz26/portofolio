— The Experience

The river does the hard work

We partner with a licensed operator on the Elo — the calmer sibling of the Progo — whose guides are certified and whose safety kayaker runs every trip. Our driver takes you to the start point and picks you up downstream with dry towels waiting.

The 12.5 km run alternates splashy rapids with slow pools under bamboo and old bridges; halfway through there's a riverside stop for young coconut and fried tempeh.

  • Certified guides plus a safety kayaker on every departure
  • Riverside break with young coconut & fried tempeh
  • Waterproof-bagged phone photos taken by your guide
